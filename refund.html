Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Cancel any time

You can cancel your subscription at any time, using the cancellation link in your payment receipt e-mail or by e-mailing us. After cancelling, no further payments are taken and you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Refunds

If you are not satisfied, you can request a full refund of your first payment within 7 days of purchase. Renewal payments are generally not refundable, but if you were charged by mistake or the Service was unavailable for a significant part of the period, contact us and we will review it fairly.

How to request a refund

E-mail nchubabria5@gmail.com from your account e-mail address, with the subject "Refund request". We reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method by our payment partner, usually within 5 to 10 business days.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a dispute with your bank. We resolve genuine issues quickly.
