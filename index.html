FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Write to us any time.

Is the app free?

Downloading and installing NIMA Studio is free. The games unlock with a subscription: $19 per month, or $192 per year (just $16 a month).

Do I need a card to download?

No. You only enter payment details when you decide to subscribe.

Does it work with OBS and TikTok LIVE Studio?

Yes. Overlays are added as a browser source in OBS or TikTok LIVE Studio using a link from the app.

Which systems are supported?

NIMA Studio is a desktop app for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit).

How do I cancel?

You can cancel at any time. Your access continues until the end of the period you have paid for. See the refund and cancellation policy.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days of your first payment, as described in our refund policy.

Is this connected to TikTok?

NIMA Studio is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

Windows shows “Unknown publisher” or “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

Yes. The app is new and not yet widely downloaded, so SmartScreen shows this for every new program. Click More info, then Run anyway.
