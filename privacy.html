Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what data NIMA STUDIO, Georgia ("we") processes when you use the NIMA Studio app and website.

Data we collect

  • Account data: your e-mail address, display name and a securely hashed password, or your Google account e-mail and name if you sign in with Google.
  • Subscription data: your plan, subscription end date and device name, used to check that your subscription is active.
  • Support messages you send us.

Data we do not collect

We do not receive or store your payment card details. Payments are processed by Dodo Payments, the merchant of record, under its own privacy policy. Your TikTok LIVE data (viewers, gifts, comments) and your game settings are processed on your own computer and are not uploaded to our servers.

How we use data

To provide and secure your account, verify your subscription, send account-related e-mails (for example a password reset code), and answer support requests. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

LIVE list on the website

While the app is connected to your TikTok LIVE and you are logged in, it sends your TikTok username and current viewer count to our server so the website can show who is live with NIMA Studio. You can switch this off at any time with the checkbox in the app sidebar. Entries disappear automatically a few minutes after you stop streaming.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run the Service (payment processing by Dodo Payments, and Google if you use Google sign-in), or when the law requires it.

Retention and your rights

We keep your account data while your account exists. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by e-mailing us. Deleting your account removes your account data, except records we must keep by law.

Security

Passwords are stored hashed, and subscription licences are digitally signed. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.
